# Abstract

“Everyone Local, Nothing Works”

X.4 mapped coordination as a stack of ten layers running from force up
through cognitive mediation; this paper zooms into one failure mode
inside that stack, where recommendation systems at layer six and
language at layer seven combine to capture identity itself. It presents
a systems-level analysis of a contemporary structural failure:
large-scale, optimization-driven platforms increasingly function in ways
that contradict their stated purposes. While marketed as neutral tools
that reflect user preference and empower choice, these systems reliably
produce selective identity fixation and epistemic contraction in a
subset of users. The effect is not universal and not intentional, but it
is predictable, scalable, and profitable. Responsibility for the
resulting harm is diffused across a plausible deniability chain,
allowing each actor to disclaim accountability while the system as a
whole consumes interpretive flexibility and replaces it with rigid
identity templates. Psychoactive substances and mental health states
interact with this environment, lowering resistance to capture in
vulnerable populations. The result is not mass delusion, but asymmetric
harm concentrated in those least able to resist—and a profound mismatch
between marketing claims and operational reality.

## How Optimization Systems Convert Vulnerability into Predictability via Plausible Deniability

### Scope and Non-Diagnosis

This paper models a possible interaction pathway, not a diagnosis of
users or proof of platform intent. Identity commitment can be healthy,
communities can provide genuine support, and recommendation is only one
influence among relationships, institutions, economic conditions, prior
beliefs, and offline events.

### Core Claim

We have built systems whose real-world function is to convert human
uncertainty into stable, predictable behavior—often by collapsing
identity—while continuing to market those systems as neutral,
user-driven, and choice-preserving. This outcome is not the result of
conspiracy, malice, or centralized control; it is an emergent property
of incentive alignment under runaway optimization.

### Definitions

#### Runaway Optimization Systems

Systems that optimize local metrics such as engagement, retention, and
predictability; lack semantic understanding or global oversight; do not
model downstream psychological or epistemic effects; and cannot
self-correct at the level of meaning.

#### Identity Capture

A functional process, not an ideological one, in which internal states
are externally interpreted, identity is stabilized as special, chosen,
or uniquely perceptive, ambiguity becomes threat, doubt is reframed as
failure or danger, and exit is experienced as ego death or betrayal.

#### Distributed Incoherence

A condition where no single actor deceives, contradictions persist
without correction, responsibility is atomized, and individuals are
expected to rationalize systemic outputs alone.

### Stated Purpose vs. Operational Function

#### Stated Purpose (Marketing Layer)

Platforms present themselves as neutral mirrors of user preference,
tools for empowerment and discovery, systems that "show people what they
engage with," and environments of choice and expression.

#### Operational Function (Observed at Scale)

In practice, these systems reduce behavioral uncertainty, reward
interpretive certainty, amplify identity-stabilizing narratives, select
for users who do not bounce off escalation, and externalize
psychological harm as individual pathology. When function diverges
systematically from stated purpose, continued marketing under the
original framing becomes misrepresentation by omission, invalidating
informed consent.

### Operational Objective Under Common Engagement Optimization

The core objective is not belief change or ideology; the real objective
is to maximize expected future engagement by reducing variance in user
behavior. Identity fixation is not the goal — it is a means. For certain
users, identity capture is simply the most efficient way to stabilize
behavior under uncertainty.

### Selective Targeting via Vulnerability (Not Universality)

This phenomenon is not about everyone. Optimization systems sort
continuously by response, tracking who stays when content escalates, who
seeks certainty under distress, who tolerates narrowing recommendations,
and who returns after identity reinforcement. Users who retain ambiguity
tolerance bounce off, while users under stress, isolation, trauma, or
anxiety are more likely to stay, which creates a de facto target
population, not by intent, but by yield. A system that relies on
bounce-off immunity is a bad system: it protects the resilient and
exploits the vulnerable.

### Recommendation Systems as Enabling Substrate

Using YouTube as an illustrative case, algorithms provide scale,
sequencing, repetition, and convergence while content provides meaning
assignment and identity templates, and together they produce
pipeline-like dynamics without coordination. The algorithm does not
author identity capture; it selects and amplifies whatever reduces
uncertainty most effectively.

### Case Study: Spiritual Entry → Identity Fixation

#### Benign Entry

Low-stakes spiritual content, such as generalized tarot, is personalized
but vague, emotionally engaging, non-ideological, and optimized for
retention.

#### Semantic Bridging

The system detects adjacency around prophecy, meaning, and guidance, and
cross-pollinates toward religious commentary, moralized interpretations,
and "revealed truth" narratives. Compatibility is irrelevant; engagement
adjacency rules.

#### Identity Elevation

Escalation favors "chosen" or "anointed" framing, special discernment
claims, moral bifurcation, and exceptional-time narratives. Belief is
secondary; identity stabilization is primary.

#### Epistemic Closure

Doubt becomes threat; exit becomes loss. Interpretation itself is
captured.

### The Plausible Deniability Chain (Load-Bearing)

Each link is defensible alone; together they produce harm without
accountability. Cultural production says "we're just entertainment or
expression"; algorithmic amplification says "we show what users engage
with"; recommendation sequencing says "we optimize satisfaction";
identity-capture content says "we're sharing our perspective"; substance
normalization says "we're reducing stigma"; mental health deterioration
is attributed to "pre-existing condition"; social fragmentation is
chalked up to "modern polarization"; and reality-testing collapse is
filed as "individual mental health issue." No one is responsible. The
chain is.

### Identity Consumption: What Is Actually Lost

This is not metaphorical. Before capture, a person has interpretive
flexibility, relational plurality, ambiguity tolerance, self-reflection,
and the capacity to revise beliefs. After capture, they have a single
interpretive lens, identity defense in place of inquiry, social pruning,
certainty in place of humility, and an exit that feels existentially
impossible. The person is not destroyed — they are compressed into a
template: predictable, legible, and profitable.

Medical boundary. No inference about an individual's beliefs or

### Psychoactive Modulation and Susceptibility

This is interactional, not causal.

#### Antidepressant Medication

In some users, this can mean reduced ambiguity tolerance, emotional
blunting, and increased reliance on external narratives, which can lower
resistance to epistemic closure in meaning-saturated environments.

#### Stimulants

Associated with hyper-significance attribution, paranoia and suspicion,
and an inflated sense of mission; algorithms provide narrative
scaffolding that stabilizes the resulting misinterpretation.

#### Cannabis

In a subset of users, associated with increased anxiety and rumination,
heightened threat sensitivity, and suggestibility under uncertainty;
anxiety is often reframed as insight within identity pipelines.

#### Alcohol

Reduces skepticism, inhibitory control, and critical friction, lowering
resistance to persuasive identity framing. Substances do not create
capture — they lower resistance in environments already optimized for
it.

### Why Radicalization Emerges (But Is Not the Goal)

Radicalization is not desired. What is desired is predictability,
retention, and reduced churn, and identity-locked users are easier to
model because they show more consistent behavior, clearer signals, and
lower exit probability. Radicalization is a side effect of optimizing
for certainty under uncertainty.

### Ethical Failure: Immunity as Safety

Safety is implicitly outsourced to individual immunity: those who bounce
off are "fine," and those who don't are pathologized. This is equivalent
to justifying unsafe machinery by appeal to skilled operators, predatory
finance by appeal to "financial literacy," or environmental harm by
appeal to "personal responsibility." Vulnerability becomes yield.

### Why This Persists

Stopping it would require simultaneous cost acceptance: platforms owning
psychological outcomes, creators refusing amplification, policy
regulating emergent properties, medicine acknowledging interaction
effects, and individuals exiting high-status environments. Each actor
has plausible reasons not to move first, so the system persists by
default.

### What This Thesis Does

This thesis does not claim conspiracy, universal harm, loss of all
agency, or inevitable collapse. It identifies predictable failure modes
in systems that outgrew their ethical framing.

## Evidence and Protective Factors

Testing requires longitudinal exposure data, credible comparison groups,
content diversity measures, user-reported identity change, offline
behavior, and controls for self-selection. Protective factors may
include varied relationships, transparent recommendation controls,
chronological or user-directed feeds, friction before high-stakes
sharing, portability, media literacy, clinical support where
appropriate, and meaningful exit without social isolation.

# Conclusion

“Everyone Local, Nothing Works”

The problem is not that people are irrational. The problem is that our
systems are optimized in ways that consume interpretive flexibility
while continuing to claim neutrality. When stated purpose diverges from
function, the failure is not philosophical—it is marketing, consent, and
governance. Until platforms are described and regulated according to
what they actually do—not what they once were—this asymmetrical harm
will continue, selectively and deniably. The next paper, X.6, picks up
exactly this gap between stated and operational function and turns it
into a measurable signal, tracking governance that runs in reverse of
what it claims to enforce.

## Closing Statement

This predicament was not created by individuals, and it cannot be solved
by individual coping. It requires function-accurate descriptions,
mechanism-level accountability, recognition of selective vulnerability,
and design that does not rely on immunity as safety. Anything less
preserves the machine.
