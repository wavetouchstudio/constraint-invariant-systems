VII.07 - Internal Coherence Under Social Pressure and Structural Hygiene

An agnostic model of identity pressure, narrative compression, and
correction-safe autonomy

# Abstract

“Agency Without Permission Slips”

This paper develops an agnostic account of conflicts historically
described as spiritual war. It locates the conflict in sustained
pressure on internal coherence: the continuity of values,
interpretations, boundaries, and self-concept under social observation.
The model distinguishes ordinary disagreement from attempts to induce
self-contradiction when difference cannot be assimilated or safely
ignored. A companion concept, structural hygiene, separates invariant
constraints, local agency, and narrative compression so that
metaphysical language does not silently become causal attribution. The
paper preserves the experiential precision of spiritual language without
requiring supernatural claims, and it adds safeguards against treating
disagreement, criticism, mental illness, or ordinary influence as
manipulation. Internal coherence is not evidence of truth or virtue; the
practical objective is correction-safe autonomy—enough stability to
revise beliefs without coerced identity fracture.

# 1. Problem and Scope

“Agency Without Permission Slips”

Spiritual-war language persists because it identifies a domain of
conflict: conscience, meaning, identity, and self-trust. Secular
translation can clarify mechanisms, but it can also truncate the
experience when it reduces every conflict to symptoms or every spiritual
description to literal entities.

The framework is descriptive and agnostic. It neither affirms nor denies
metaphysical interpretations. It asks when social pressure shifts from
argument about claims or behavior toward non-consensual destabilization
of the person's capacity to interpret and revise their own experience.

# 2. Definitions

“Agency Without Permission Slips”

**Internal coherence.** Relative consistency among an individual's
values, interpretations, boundaries, and self-concept across time.
Coherence does not imply accuracy, goodness, or immunity to revision.

**Social observation.** Interpersonal, familial, institutional,
professional, or mediated contexts in which identity and behavior are
interpreted and responded to.

**Manipulation.** Pressure that seeks to alter another person's
interpretation or action while obscuring relevant intent, evidence,
alternatives, or exit. The term requires more than disagreement or
persuasion.

**Narrative compression.** A symbolic account that makes experience
communicable by omitting detail. Narratives can guide action without
being identical to underlying structure.

**Structural hygiene.** A discipline of keeping constraint, causation,
agency, narrative, evidence, and metaphor from being substituted for one
another.

**Correction-safe autonomy.** The capacity to preserve self-authorship
while remaining open to evidence, consent-based influence, and
non-punitive revision.

# 3. Three-Phase Mechanism

“Agency Without Permission Slips”

1.  Stable internal organization: the person maintains a workable
    self-model, boundaries, and interpretive independence without
    requiring others to agree.

2.  Non-assimilation becomes salient: surrounding actors or systems
    experience the person's difference as difficult to categorize,
    mirror, govern, or absorb.

3.  Pressure substitutes for coexistence: reframing, guilt, fear,
    pathologization, or narrative substitution targets the person's
    authority over internal meaning.

This sequence is a proposed pattern, not a presumption of motive.
Similar behavior can arise from misunderstanding, care, institutional
duty, cultural mismatch, or reciprocal escalation. Attribution requires
evidence about persistence, consent, power, exit, and response to
correction.

# 4. Disagreement, Influence, and Destabilization

| **Interaction** | **Typical features** | **Correction test** |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Good-faith disagreement | Claims and evidence remain contestable; difference may persist | Both parties can restate, revise, or disengage |
| Consensual influence | Persuasion is visible and alternatives remain available | Consent can be withdrawn without identity punishment |

| Identity destabilization | Pressure targets self-trust or interpretive autonomy beyond the disputed claim | Exit and correction are blocked, punished, or recursively reframed |

# 5. Why Spiritual Language Persists

“Agency Without Permission Slips”

The word spiritual can preserve the felt location of the conflict even
when its ontology is disputed. It signals that pressure concerns
meaning, conscience, belonging, and identity rather than only visible
conduct. The language is useful when held as phenomenological
description; it becomes risky when metaphor is converted into proof of
hidden entities, coordinated enemies, or moral superiority.

A structurally hygienic translation can therefore preserve both
vocabularies: the experience may be described spiritually, while causal
claims remain subject to ordinary evidentiary standards.

# 6. Structure, Agency, and Narrative

“Agency Without Permission Slips”

Table 2. Layers that structural hygiene keeps distinct

| **Layer** | **Role** | **Common substitution** |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Constraint | Defines or changes possible action | Treated as an intentional actor |
| Causation | Contributes to an event or outcome | Collapsed into blame or moral agency |
| Agency | Selects, authorizes, maintains, revises, or refuses | Exported upward to history, systems, algorithms, or divinity |
| Narrative | Compresses experience into communicable meaning | Mistaken for complete structure or dismissed as meaningless |
| Metaphor | Transfers relations across domains | Presented as literal mechanism or evidence |

The precursor or creation role used elsewhere in the manuscript should
be treated as a proposed structural referent: enabling conditions are
not event-level authors. Denying metaphysical agency does not require
assigning total agency to history, biology, capital, algorithms, or
institutions.

# 7. Language Failure and Metaphysical Drift

“Agency Without Permission Slips”

Discrete language compresses gradients into categories such as
exists/does not exist, voluntary/coerced, healthy/pathological, or
aligned/misaligned. These binaries can clarify decisions, but they can
also conceal mixed causation, partial agency, and uncertainty.

Metaphysical drift is not automatically proof that a model has revealed
an invariant. It may reflect ambiguity, rhetorical priming, cultural
inheritance, or an underdefined term. Structural hygiene requires the
model to disclose those alternatives rather than treating
misinterpretation as confirmation.

# 8. Longitudinal and Abstracted Harm

“Agency Without Permission Slips”

Pressure on internal coherence can accumulate through repeated
reframing, inherited narratives, inconsistent rules, and punishment of
correction. Harm may be distributed across many low-intensity
interactions rather than a single overt event.

- Duration and recurrence matter more than one isolated disagreement.

- Power, dependency, and exit cost affect whether influence is
  meaningfully voluntary.

- Observable effects should be separated from assumptions about intent.

- Targets may also misinterpret care, criticism, or incompatibility as
  manipulation.

- Independent records and third-party review can reduce recursive
  disputes about reality.

# 9. Structural Hygiene Protocol

“Agency Without Permission Slips”

1.  Name the disputed claim, behavior, boundary, or decision precisely.

2.  Separate observed events from interpretations, metaphors, and
    inferred motives.

3.  Map power, dependency, information, alternatives, and exit costs.

4.  Test whether disagreement can persist without identity punishment.

5.  Check whether each side can revise, pause, or seek independent
    review.

6.  Use the least metaphysically loaded explanation that preserves the
    relevant experience.

7.  Retain uncertainty when evidence cannot distinguish manipulation
    from mismatch or reciprocal escalation.

# 10. Alignment Without Belief Enforcement

“Agency Without Permission Slips”

Alignment is defined here provisionally as coherent navigation of
constraints, not doctrinal obedience. People with incompatible
metaphysical narratives can still act responsibly, compassionately, and
predictably. Shared action does not require agreement about the ultimate
source of constraint.

The stronger claim that coherence automatically reduces friction or
produces moral convergence remains a hypothesis. Coherent actors may
pursue incompatible goals, and internally coherent systems can be
harmful. Outcomes, consent, reciprocity, and correction remain necessary
tests.

# 11. Revelation, Iteration, and Detractors

“Agency Without Permission Slips”

The source reframes revelation as local recognition of a pre-existing
pattern rather than supernatural interruption. Used cautiously, this can
describe a compression event in which ambiguity falls enough to make
coordinated action possible. It does not establish that the recognized
pattern is true, universal, or historically inevitable.

1.  Constraint or pattern recognition

2.  Local experimentation and provisional alignment

3.  Coordination amplification

4.  Narrative formation

5.  Compression drift and institutionalization

6.  Renewed recognition, correction, or fork

Detractors are expected in plural systems because language remains
lossy, alignment is voluntary, and learning is asynchronous. Opposition
can also identify genuine errors. A system is not resilient because
criticism cannot break it; it is resilient when criticism can be
processed without coercive convergence or total collapse.

# 12. Boundaries and Non-Examples

“Agency Without Permission Slips”

- Voluntary belief change or ordinary self-doubt

- Good-faith criticism supported by reasons and evidence

- Mutual influence with revocable consent

- Clinical concern evaluated through appropriate evidence and safeguards

- Consequences for harmful behavior that preserve due process

- Temporary conflict or incompatibility where disengagement remains
  available

The framework must not be used to dismiss all criticism as identity
attack, to replace clinical assessment, or to certify the narrator's
account without corroboration.

# 14. Limits and Research Needs

“Agency Without Permission Slips”

- Internal coherence is difficult to measure and can coexist with false
  beliefs.

- Accounts of manipulation are vulnerable to asymmetric information and
  reciprocal attribution.

- Cultural norms differ in how autonomy, family obligation,
  spirituality, and disagreement are expressed.

- The three-phase mechanism requires empirical comparison with ordinary
  conflict and coercive-control models.

- Claims about civilizational phases, recurring revelation, and moral
  convergence remain theoretical.

# 15. Conclusion

“Agency Without Permission Slips”

Internal-coherence conflict names a real class of experiences without
deciding their metaphysical interpretation in advance. The model becomes
useful when it identifies persistent pressure on self-authorship,
blocked exit, and substitution of manipulation for claim-level
engagement.

Structural hygiene keeps that insight from becoming another closed
narrative. It separates observation from interpretation, constraint from
agency, metaphor from mechanism, and coherence from truth. The aim is
not invulnerability to influence; it is correction-safe autonomy—the
ability to remain a person while learning, disagreeing, revising, or
leaving.

## Key Contributions

- An agnostic three-phase model of pressure on internal coherence.

- A distinction among disagreement, consensual influence, protective
  intervention, and identity destabilization.

- A structural-hygiene protocol separating constraint, causation,
  agency, narrative, and metaphor.

- A qualified account of spiritual language, revelation, and recurring
  narrative cycles.

- Safeguards against metaphysical inflation, criticism refusal, clinical
  substitution, and one-sided attribution.
